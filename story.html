A mother's recipe.A son's promise.

How Christian took the lumpia he grew up on and turned it into Rhea's Rolls, so his mom's cooking could keep feeding people for years to come.

1The kitchen table

It started with her hands.

Christian grew up watching his mom roll lumpia. Bowls of filling on the counter, a stack of wrappers under a damp towel, and a tray that filled up faster than you'd think possible. Every birthday, every holiday, every Sunday with family, her lumpia was there.

He learned by sitting next to her: how much filling goes in, how to tuck the ends, how tight to roll so it fries up crisp and never bursts. Those lessons never came from a recipe card. They came from years at her table.

2Picking up the wrapper

Keeping her legacy rolling.

Rhea's Rolls is how Christian makes sure that table never goes quiet. He rolls every lumpia the way his mom taught him, by hand, one at a time, with no machines and no shortcuts.

What started as rolling for friends and family turned into thousands of orders. More than 9,000 lumpia later, Christian still treats every batch like it's going to his mom's own party.

3Building something real

All in, every batch.

Christian isn't building a side hustle. He's building a company he's proud of, from late nights prepping filling to early mornings setting up at the market. He loves seeing someone take their first bite of real Filipino lumpia, and he loves it even more when they come back the next week with friends.

Every order, every market, and every catering tray is a step toward something bigger: making Rhea's Rolls a name people across Massachusetts know and love.

“When people taste my lumpia, they're tasting my mom's cooking. That's what I'm proud to share.”

Christian, founder of Rhea's Rolls

What we bring to the table

Filipino food is about gathering. Rhea's Rolls brings that same feeling to our corner of Massachusetts.

  • A real taste of home

    For Filipino families here, it's the lumpia they grew up with and can't always find nearby. For everyone else, it's a delicious first introduction to Filipino food.

  • Life at the night market

    We set up at local night markets and community events, frying fresh rolls on the spot and bringing people together around food.

  • A seat for everyone

    From birthdays to fundraisers, our trays show up for the moments that bring neighbors together. Local, family-run, and here to stay.
